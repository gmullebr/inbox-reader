Inbox Reader

A private app for one household

Inbox Reader shows the threads in a person's own Gmail inbox on their iPhone and Mac, and archives a thread in Gmail when that person asks. It has no function that sends, replies to or forwards email.

Only the members of one household use the app. Nobody can download it from an app store, and the household installs it on its own devices. Each person signs in with their own Google account and can end the app's access at any time.

Inbox Reader terms of use

Last changed on 25 October 2026

Inbox Reader serves the members of one household, on their own devices and with their own Google accounts.

1Who may use it

The household member who runs the app invites the other members. The app has no public sign-up and no listing in any app store.

2What it does

The app reads the user's own Gmail inbox and archives threads in Gmail at the user's request. It has no function that sends, replies to or forwards email. Gmail stays the record: an archived thread remains in Gmail under All Mail.

3Warranty

The household provides the app as it is, without any warranty. The privacy policy describes how the app handles data.

4Ending use

A user stops by signing out in the app, or by removing its access in their Google account at myaccount.google.com/connections. The household member who runs the app may stop it at any time.

5Changes

The date at the top of this page shows the last change.
